Privacy policy

How NILRSF handles personal data and privacy requests.

This policy describes the limited data practices for the NILRSF informational website and explains how visitors can contact the privacy point of contact.

1. Scope and purpose

This website is an informational and institutional initiative. It does not require user accounts, does not process payments, and does not collect sensitive personal data beyond what is necessary for simple contact or basic site functionality.

2. Data collected

Depending on the interaction, the site may collect:

  • Contact information submitted via email correspondence or a future contact mechanism, if used.
  • Basic browser and session information needed to operate the website, such as consent choices and local preferences.
  • Analytics or marketing data only if the visitor expressly allows those options in the consent banner.

3. Purpose and legal basis

The purpose of processing personal data is limited to responding to inquiries, improving the website, and administering consent settings. The legal basis for strictly necessary processing is the legitimate need to provide and secure the website. Where analytics or marketing tools are enabled, the legal basis depends on the visitor’s express consent.

4. Retention and third parties

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the stated purpose, to comply with applicable recordkeeping obligations, or to satisfy a legal request. No third-party processor is currently required for core site operation. If analytics services are enabled in the future, they will be disclosed here and applied only after consent.

5. Data subject rights

Visitors may request access, correction, anonymization, or deletion of personal data by contacting the privacy point of contact at andrea29alonso@gmail.com. The site operator will acknowledge the request and respond within a reasonable timeframe, taking into account the nature and volume of the request.

6. Privacy contact

For privacy matters, including requests under applicable data protection laws, please contact the privacy/data contact at andrea29alonso@gmail.com.

7. International visitors

Visitors from outside Brazil may have additional rights under applicable regional laws. This notice is a general informational statement and should be reviewed and finalized by qualified legal counsel for a specific legal or operational context.
