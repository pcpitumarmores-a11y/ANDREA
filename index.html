About the framework

Institutional governance to improve security, transparency, and integrity.

Design rationale

Institutional governance model to improve security, transparency, and integrity of U.S. real estate transactions by standardizing document verification, identity validation, risk assessment, and transaction monitoring across notaries, financial institutions, title companies, auctioneers, attorneys, and public recording offices.

Project objectives

Core focus areas for institutional integrity and governance.

  • Preventing fraud and financial crime: strengthening the control environment around high-value transactions.
  • Strengthening transaction integrity: improving the reliability and traceability of official process steps.
  • Improving stakeholder coordination: aligning public and private actors through a common governance structure.
  • Protecting property rights and public land records: supporting the reliability of records that underpin ownership and transfer.
  • Enhancing regulatory compliance: reinforcing procedural accountability and institutional discipline.
  • Reducing operational inefficiencies: improving clarity and consistency across document and identity processes.
  • Building institutional knowledge and professional capacity: supporting training and continuity across organizations.
  • Creating a scalable and replicable governance model: enabling adoption beyond a single pilot or jurisdiction.
